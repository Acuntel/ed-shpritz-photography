# MONTHLY BONUS — WHAT FUN!! / Find 3 to Win

## Status

Pre-launch. The public Bonus page may show the teaser, but the entry form,
official rules, dates, and public announcement stay off until the complete
test pass is finished.

## Purpose

Invite visitors to explore the website closely, read Ed’s descriptions, and
find three photographs in different collections.

## Game

- Name: **WHAT FUN!! — Find 3 to Win**
- Prize: one **2027 Ed Shpritz Photography calendar**
- Entry: name, email, and the three photograph numbers
- Winning method: first verified, complete, correct, eligible entry received
- Winner contact: email; winner has 72 hours to respond

## Before turning on entries

- Set the exact start date, closing date/time, and Central Time.
- Confirm age and geographic eligibility, prize value, shipping, and sponsor
  contact details.
- Have the final official rules reviewed for the jurisdictions where entry is
  allowed.
- Build entry storage with timestamps, duplicate-entry handling, a confirmation
  screen/email, and notifications for Ed.
- Link the entry form to the rules and privacy information.
- Test correct, wrong, blank, duplicate, desktop, iPhone, iPad, confirmation,
  timestamp, deadline closing, and public links before announcing.

## Technical build path

The website already has encrypted private record storage and a passcode-
protected Admin list. The contest will use the same proven pattern, but its
entries must stay separate from newsletter subscribers.

- Store each contest entry privately: name, email, B 02 / LO18 / WL 08 answers,
  timestamp, entry number, and verification/winner status.
- Use a hidden spam-trap field and reject duplicate email entries without
  exposing prior entries.
- Show the contest list only inside Admin, with a simple download option for
  Ed.
- Use the website’s existing protected mail service for the entrant’s receipt
  and for Ed’s entry notification; test both before launch.
- Enforce the published Central Time deadline on the server, not just on the
  visible page.

## Facebook and Instagram safeguards

Facebook and Instagram are promotion posts only; the website is the only place
to enter, read the rules, and provide information.

- Keep each social post to one photograph or simple graphic, a short teaser,
  and the website link (or “link in bio” on Instagram).
- Never make liking, following, sharing, tagging, commenting, or messaging a
  condition of entry or an extra chance to win.
- Do not use Facebook or Instagram logos, screenshots, or wording that could
  imply either platform sponsors or endorses the challenge.
- Put the final rules and entry page on the website, including the standard
  statement that the promotion is not sponsored, endorsed, administered by, or
  associated with Facebook or Instagram.
- Use “Instagram” in final public copy, not abbreviated brand names.
- Do not publish or buy an ad until the final dates, rules, privacy wording,
  and entry experience have been reviewed together.
